# Contributing to Apatch

Apatch accepts contributions under the same MIT terms as the project
(inbound = outbound), unless a file or contribution is explicitly covered by
different terms before it is submitted.

Every commit contributed for inclusion must carry a `Signed-off-by:` line.
That sign-off certifies the Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1 published at
<https://developercertificate.org/>.

Contributors are responsible for confirming that they may submit the code,
documentation, tests, generated material, and data in their contribution.
Record relevant source and generation provenance, including material produced
with automated assistance. Do not submit private, confidential, or
third-party material without the required permission and attribution.

Personal and workspace extensions are separate artifacts. Their authors choose
their terms in the extension manifest; using Apatch's public out-of-process
extension contract does not transfer ownership or publication rights to the
Apatch project. Apatch Pro is distributed separately under its own terms.
